Welcome to Stellar Mandate!

This is a big learning project for me. I am hoping to build a pretty full-function game for one player to start with.
Then, I will use it as a launching pad to learn about user sign-in, multiplayer, and monetization down the road.

Inspired by apps like Atlas Earth and Terramine, I want to explore the world of play-to-earn design.
---------------------------------

In Stellar Mandate, players compete to win influence over vast regions in space. Influence is gained by deploying
forces belonging to one of seven different factions onto planets within each region. Factions have rock-paper-scissors-
style advantages and disadvantages against each other, and a savvy commander uses the right factions at the right locations
to gain the largest amount of influence over the competition.

- Deploy faction forces to planets to gain influence
- Earn credits to buy more forces
- Dominate your rivals with strategy and timing
